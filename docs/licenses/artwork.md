# Artwork used in the RE-ANIMATOR window

All artwork in the app is our own (drawn 2026-10-26, Claude, for Dennis):

* `design/reanimator-cover.png` and `reanimator/assets/banner.png`: the RE-ANIMATOR cover (glowing bars, white title),
  inspired by the look of Maschine 3's artwork but not copied from it.
* `design/reanimator-repair-badge.png` → `reanimator/assets/right-to-repair.png`: "REPAIR · DON'T REPLACE" badge
  (circular arrow around a wrench). It refers to the right-to-repair idea without using that campaign's logo.
* `design/reanimator-ewaste-ring.png` → `reanimator/assets/e-waste-ring.png`: recycling arrows around a small pad
  controller. It refers to e-waste recycling without using the stock illustration.

`design/reference/` holds the pictures that inspired the two badges (the Right to Repair campaign logo and an "E-WASTE
RECYCLING" stock illustration). They are reference only: not in the app, not to be distributed.

## The MK1 screen animation (bridge/anim/make_anim.py)

All of it is our own drawing, inspired by the film *Re-Animator*; nothing is traced or cut from a poster, cover or
still. A scientist portrait in Jeffrey Combs's likeness (Dennis reported on 2026-10-27 that Jeffrey Combs said this is fine)
was tried and **removed the same day**: no likeness is in the animation now. The film's posters and DVD covers
belong to their artists and publishers and are not used.
